# Weekly Page (Sunday, about 10 min)

Read last week's page before writing this one.

```text
Week of ______   Played ___   Reviewed ___   W ___  L ___  D ___
TALLY   H ____  M ____  T ____  K ____  C ____  O ____  E ____
Top tag ___   Same as last week? Y / N   Last week's rule kept? Y / N
Found  ✓ ___   ✗ ___     Struck ___     Fast (<10 s) ___ of ___ rows
Maia game #___   N ___  L ___  X ___  R ___
Rule for next week: __________________________________________________
Drill for next week: _________________________________________________
```

- **Reviewed** counts games with at least hot notes and a CHK line. Reviewed / Played is the consistency score.
- **Tally, Fast** come from kept rows only. **✓, ✗, Struck** come from the CHK lines.
- Bot games and lite reviews stay out of the tally and the ✓/✗ count.

## Choosing the drill

| Signal this week | Drill |
| --- | --- |
| Lots of H or M | Hanging-piece puzzles, plus "can anything take it?" before every move |
| Lots of T | Tactics puzzles (forks, pins) |
| Lots of C, or Fast is over a third of rows | 30 seconds minimum on any move that captures or checks |
| Lots of E | Basic winning endgames |
| ✗ outnumbers ✓ | Slow Step 2 down: stop on every capture and check, not just material swings |
| Mostly R (Maia) | Process: tighten the blunder check and use more clock |
| Mostly N (Maia) | Puzzles on that theme and replays of those positions against Maia |

## Monthly check

Take the last 10 losses. For each, take the row that decided it: the ★ row if there is one, else the result-changer. Count those 10 tags and spend most of next month's study time on the leading one. Keep opening study a small share.
