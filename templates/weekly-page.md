## Choosing the drill

- Lots of **H or M:** hanging-piece puzzles, plus a "can anything take it?" check before every move.
- Lots of **T:** tactics puzzles (forks, pins).
- Lots of **C:** spend at least 30 seconds on any move involving a capture or check.
- Lots of **E:** practice basic winning endgames.
- Mostly **R** (Maia): process is the bottleneck; tighten the blunder check and use more clock.
- Mostly **N** (Maia): weight toward puzzles on that theme and replays of those positions.

## Monthly check

Take the last 10 losses, find the mistake that decided each, sort by tag, and spend most of next month's study time on the leading category. Keep opening study a small share.
