# Per-Game Entry (half a page)

```text
#__  Date ____  W/B  Result W/L/D  Opp ____  Opening ________
Clock left (me/opp): ___ / ___
HOT: Turned at move __ because ___________________________
     Feeling / clock: ____________________________________
MOMENTS (max 3)
 Mv | Played & why     | What I missed  | Better move | Tag | Eng ✓/✗ | Maia
 __ | ________________ | ______________ | ___________ | ___ | ___     | ___
 __ | ________________ | ______________ | ___________ | ___ | ___     | ___
 __ | ________________ | ______________ | ___________ | ___ | ___     | ___
ENGINE: big swings I didn't find: _________________________
NEXT TIME I WILL: ________________________________________
```

- **HOT** lines are written right after the game, before any engine.
- **Moments:** up to 3. In a loss, include the first moment material was lost.
- **Tag:** H / M / T / K / C / O / E (see `tag-key.md`).
- **Eng:** ✓ found myself, ✗ engine-only.
- **Maia:** N / L / X / R, filled only for the game analyzed in the weekly Maia session.

```text
ENGINE: 22...Qxb2 found only by engine (+0.3 → −2.8).
        Ignored 5 small changes under 1 point.
NEXT TIME I WILL: before moving a piece, ask "can a PAWN take it there?"
```
