# Per-Game Entry (half a page)

Rule the page once, then copy this layout for every game. The double bar splits what I write in Step 2 (left) from what I write in Step 3 (right). See `../docs/01-review-process.md` for the steps and rules.

```text
#_________  W/B  W/L/D how ______   vs __________ (____)  me ____
Opening _____________________________   Clock me/opp ____ / ____
HOT  Turned at move ___: _____________________________________________
     Felt / clock: ___________________________________________________
  Mv | I played … because | Bad because     | Better … because ||<10s|Tag|✓✗|Maia
 ___ | __________________ | _______________ | ________________ || __ | _ | _ | _
 ___ | __________________ | _______________ | ________________ || __ | _ | _ | _
 ___ | __________________ | _______________ | ________________ || __ | _ | _ | _
CHK  flags ___   ✓ ___   ✗ ___   struck ___
NEXT TIME I WILL _____________________________________________________
```

| Field | Step | What to write |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `#` | 1 | Match ID: date as YYMMDD, then game of the day (`261027-2`). Matches the chesster file's title; the monthly check refers to it |
| W/B, W/L/D | 1 | My colour, result |
| how | 1 | mate / resign / time / draw type |
| vs, (rating), me | 2 | Opponent's name and rating, my rating, from the game page |
| Opening, Clock | 2 | Opening name; clock left for me and them at the end |
| HOT | 1 | Two lines from memory only: where it turned; feeling and clock |
| Mv | 2 or 3 | Move number. ★ in front for the first material lost in a loss |
| I played … because | 2 or 3 | My move and my reason at the time, even for a ✗ row |
| Bad because | 2 or 3 | What it allowed or missed, one phrase. "?" if I can't tell |
| Better … because | 2 or 3 | The move and why. "engine idea – skip" if I can't explain it in about 60 s |
| <10 s | 3 | Tick if chesster's Time line shows under 10 seconds |
| Tag | 3 | One of H M T K C O E (see `tag-key.md`) |
| ✓✗ | 3 | ✓ I had it in Step 2, ✗ chesster only, – struck row |
| Maia | weekly | N L X R, only for the week's Maia game |
| CHK | 3 | All of chesster's flags, my ✓ and ✗ counts, rows struck |
| NEXT TIME I WILL | 4 | One action rule; or KEEP: one habit that worked |

- **Moments:** up to 3, chosen in the order ★ first material lost → result-changer → ✗ flags → the rest.
- **Struck rows** (my moments chesster didn't flag) stay on the page with a line through them. Write a fourth or fifth row below the three if needed; it still gets struck or kept.
- **Lite or short version:** write it next to the match ID (`#261027-2 lite`). Lite rows get no ✓/✗.

## Filled-in example (invented)

```text
#261027-1  B  L how resign   vs pawnstorm88 (742)  me 790
Opening Italian Game                  Clock me/opp 4:05 / 7:50
HOT  Turned at move 14: lost my knight, never got back in it
     Felt / clock: fine until 14, played Nd4 fast, rushed after
  Mv | I played … because | Bad because     | Better … because ||<10s|Tag|✓✗|Maia
 ★14 | Nd4, hits queen    | c3 pawn takes   | Be7, develop     || ✔  | H | ✓ | R
  19 | a6, "waiting"      | Qxf7+ coming    | Qe7, guards f7   ||    | M | ✓ | N
  22 | Rd8, contest file  | missed Qxb2     | Qxb2, free rook  ||    | T | ✗ | N
  9  | h6, felt loose     | ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ | ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ ||    |   | – |
CHK  flags 3   ✓ 2   ✗ 1   struck 1
NEXT TIME I WILL before moving a piece, ask "can a PAWN take it there?"
```

**Reading it:** chesster flagged 3 moves; I had 2 of them, it found 22...Qxb2 on its own, and my move-9 worry wasn't a swing. 14...Nd4 was fast and an R: a process slip, which the rule targets. Tally: H, M, T.
